Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

JPG rclone backup ("the app") is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

The app accesses Google Drive only to upload, list, update and remove its own backup files in the owner's Drive.

What the app collects and shares

The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data. No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's server.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
